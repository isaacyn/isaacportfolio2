01 / ABOUT

A scientific mindset.
A builder’s approach.

I’m building a career that joins the science of materials and industrial processes with the problem-solving power of technology.

My Industrial Chemistry degree at Chuka University gives me a foundation in analytical, organic, inorganic and physical chemistry. In 2026 I completed an industrial attachment in the Quality Control laboratory of National Cement Co. Ltd, Lukenya Grinding Unit, where I worked with gravimetric and titrimetric methods, XRF analysis and cement physical testing.

Alongside the laboratory, I develop skills in Python, web development, electronics and Linux. I value careful experimentation, clear documentation and learning by building.
